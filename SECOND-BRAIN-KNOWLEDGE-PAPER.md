A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. B. du Boulay (1986). Some Difficulties of Learning to Program. Journal of Educational Computing Research — Notional-machine / mental-model framework for programming pedagogy.
2. D. Parsons, P. Haden (2006). Parson's Programming Puzzles: A Fun and Effective Learning Tool for First Programming Courses. ACE Conference — Origin of Parson's Problems technique.
3. J. Sweller (1988). Cognitive Load During Problem Solving: Effects on Learning. Cognitive Science — Worked-example effect foundational research.
4. R. Lister et al. (2004). A Multi-National Study of Reading and Tracing Skills in Novice Programmers. ITiCSE — Empirical basis for code-tracing pedagogy.
5. B. Becker et al. (2019). Compiler Error Messages Considered Unhelpful: The Landscape of Text-Based Programming Error Message Research. ITiCSE — Error-message literacy research.
6. M. Guzdial (2015). Learner-Centered Design of Computing Education: Research on Computing for Everyone. Morgan & Claypool — CS-education research synthesis.
7. A. Robins, J. Rountree, N. Rountree (2003). Learning and Teaching Programming: A Review and Discussion. Computer Science Education — Comprehensive review of programming-pedagogy research.
8. M. Kölling, B. Quig, A. Patterson, J. Rosenberg (2003). The BlueJ System and Its Pedagogy. Computer Science Education — Applied pedagogy for beginner programming environments.
9. C. Kelleher, R. Pausch (2005). Lowering the Barriers to Programming: A Taxonomy of Programming Environments and Languages for Novice Programmers. ACM Computing Surveys — Taxonomy of learner-appropriate tooling.
10. E. Soloway, J. Spohrer (1989). Studying the Novice Programmer. Erlbaum — Foundational novice-programmer cognition research.
11. S. Loksa, A. Ko (2016). The Role of Self-Regulation in Programming Problem Solving Process and Success. ICER — Metacognitive strategies in programming learning.
12. D. Kirschner, J. Sweller, R. Clark (2006). Why Minimal Guidance During Instruction Does Not Work. Educational Psychologist — Support for structured guided instruction in technical education.
13. T. Fullerton (2014). Game Design Workshop (3rd ed.). CRC Press — Project-based iterative practice methodology (cross-applicable to coding projects).
14. S. Papert (1980). Mindstorms: Children, Computers, and Powerful Ideas. Basic Books — Constructionist learning-by-building philosophy underlying PBL.
15. M. Ben-Ari (2001). Constructivism in Computer Science Education. Journal of Computers in Mathematics and Science Teaching — Constructivist framework for CS pedagogy.
16. J. Sorva (2013). Notional Machines and Introductory Programming Education. ACM Transactions on Computing Education — Extended notional-machine framework.
17. B. Simon et al. (2008). Predictors of Success in a First Programming Course. ACE Conference — Empirical predictors informing diagnostic-assessment design.
18. A. Luxton-Reilly et al. (2018). Introductory Programming: A Systematic Literature Review. ITiCSE Companion — Comprehensive review of intro-programming pedagogy research.
19. M. Piech, J. Huang, A. Nguyen, M. Phulsuksombati, M. Sahami, L. Guibas (2015). Learning Program Embeddings to Propagate Feedback on Student Code. ICML — ML-informed insight into common student code patterns/errors.
20. D. Bouvier, E. Lovellette, J. Matta et al. (2016). Novice Programmers and the Problem Description Effect. SIGCSE — Research on how problem-description clarity affects learner success.
